SCREENSHOT
==========

WordPress shows the file `screenshot.png` from a theme's root folder as the
theme thumbnail on Appearance > Themes. We could not generate a real PNG in
this build, so please add one before shipping.

Required file:  themes/dog-father-child/screenshot.png
Recommended size: 1200 x 900 px (WordPress displays it at 4:3; 1200x900 is the
modern high-DPI standard and matches the WordPress.org recommendation).

INTENDED LOOK
-------------
A single, premium "hero" frame that communicates the brand at a glance:

  - Deep black (#000000) background.
  - A large luxury photograph of a happy dog in a five-star resort setting
    (soft, warm lighting), or the gold paw / crown logo on black.
  - The wordmark "THE DOG FATHER" in Poppins, white, with a gold (#FEC208)
    or primary-yellow (#FFF10A) accent.
  - One gold "Book Now" pill button to hint at the call-to-action style.
  - Generous negative space — Apple/Tesla-level simplicity. No clutter.

Export as PNG (or optimized JPG renamed to .png), keep it under ~500 KB.
